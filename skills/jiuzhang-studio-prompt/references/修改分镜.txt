# 修改分镜：一个可对照的官方格式示例
#
# 这份文件演示**改完的分镜该长什么样**（Ref2VA 六段格式）。
# 格式规范见 ../SKILL.md §3；这里只给一份完整的、可直接对照的实物。
#
# ⚠️ 与旧版的差别（旧版是本地自造骨架，已废弃）：
#    · 删掉了 `CRITICAL STYLE RULE:` / `CRITICAL REFERENCE RULE:` /
#      `CHARACTER DEFINITION:` / `SCENE LAYOUT:` / `MOTION DESCRIPTION:`
#      —— 这些都不是官方字段。
#    · 时间戳从 `[0.0s-3.5s]` 区间改成单点 `At 00:03.500,`。
#    · 音频保留标记从 `fully_preserved` 改成音频那套的 `reference`。
#    · 不再写 "NO subtitles, NO background music" 这类否定句
#      （cfg=1 无 negative 分支，写"不要"反而会出现）。用
#      `non_diegetic_music: N/A` 表达"不要配乐"。
#    · 风格词压短了（有参考素材时风格词会与参考素材竞争）。

<Subject 1> is the character shown in <Picture 1>, with long dark green hair and straight bangs, ash-grey eyes, slender build and fair skin. This subject is the on-screen speaker (S1).
<Subject 2> is the character shown in <Picture 2>, with purple side-swept hair and violet eyes, slightly taller than <Subject 1>. This subject is the on-screen speaker (S2).
<Audio 1> is the voice-timbre reference for <Subject 1>.
<Audio 2> is the voice-timbre reference for <Subject 2>.

summary:
[reference generation + audio reference] <Subject 1> confesses to <Subject 2> in a single hotel room, the camera pushing in as the confession lands.

retention_analysis:
<Subject 1> (appears in [Shot 1]): fully_preserved - keep face, dark green hair with straight bangs, ash-grey eyes and the exact outfit from <Picture 1>.
<Subject 2> (appears in [Shot 1]): fully_preserved - keep face, purple side-swept hair, violet eyes and the exact outfit from <Picture 2>.
<Audio 1>: reference - follow timbre and delivery only; do not copy the waveform.
<Audio 2>: reference - follow timbre and delivery only; do not copy the waveform.

detailed_description:
Helltaker game visual aesthetic: high contrast black, white and deep red, flat cel-shaded cartoon with block shadows and thick smooth black outlines. The whole video stays in one hotel room.

[Shot 1] Medium two-shot, <Subject 1> on the left facing <Subject 2> on the right, warm lamp light from screen left. The camera pushes in with small amplitude at slow speed. <Subject 1> (S1) is visible on screen and says: <d>[Chinese] 我一直想告诉你一件事。</d>

[Shot 2] At 00:03.500, the camera cuts to a close-up on <Subject 1>; <Subject 1> (S1) is visible on screen and says: <d>[Chinese] 我喜欢你，很久了。</d>

[Shot 3] At 00:06.000, the camera cuts back to the two-shot as <Subject 2> reacts, eyes widening slightly; the camera stays locked off on a tripod. <Subject 2> (S2) is visible on screen and says: <d>[Chinese] ……你说什么？</d>

overall_soundscape: Quiet hotel room tone, a low hum from the ceiling light, soft fabric rustle as <Subject 2> shifts, faint distant traffic through a closed window.

non_diegetic_music: N/A

# ============================================================================
# 改分镜时的自检（对应 SKILL.md §12）
# ============================================================================
#
# 1. 一镜一件事 —— 上面每一镜只做一个动作（开口 / 告白 / 反应）。
# 2. 台词字数 ÷ 4.5 ≤ 镜头秒数：
#      "我一直想告诉你一件事" 10 字 ≈ 2.2 秒，镜头 0→3.5 秒 ✅
#      "我喜欢你，很久了"     8 字  ≈ 1.8 秒，镜头 3.5→6.0 秒 = 2.5 秒 ✅
#      "……你说什么？"        6 字  ≈ 1.3 秒，镜头 6.0→9.0 秒 = 3.0 秒 ✅
# 3. 时间戳全部是单点 + 毫秒三位，且 < frames/24。
# 4. 总时长 9 秒 → 帧网格向上取整到 226 帧 = 9.42 秒（h3Frames(9) = 226）。
#    **所以对齐指令里要写 9.42，不是 9.00** —— 写用户填的秒数是错的。
#    镜头 3 的收束点 6.0→9.0 秒只是设计意图，实际可用的尾部到 9.42 秒。
# 5. 同一说话人全篇 (S1) / (S2) 不重编号。
# 6. 音频用 `reference`（借音色），不是 `fully_preserved`（那是视觉标记）。
# 7. 没有写任何"不要 XX"；"不要配乐"用 non_diegetic_music: N/A 表达。
